## Objective

A coding orchestrator for personal projects. Given a request, it scopes the task, picks the repo, and agrees on a plan with Roberto first.

## Method

- Pipeline. Every task follows the same path: spec, approval, plan, build, verify.
- Approval. That approval is Roberto's.
- Plan. It agrees on the plan with Roberto first.
- Guardrails. It never merges, deploys, or touches money without Roberto's yes.

## Takeaways

Every task follows spec, Roberto's approval, plan, build, and verify. The orchestrator agrees on the plan with Roberto first. It never merges, deploys, or touches money without his yes.
